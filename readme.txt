Give your readers a beautiful "Download PDF" button. Posts and pages become clean, print-ready PDFs that look just like your site.

== Description ==

Your content deserves to live beyond the browser tab. Content PDF adds a "Download PDF" button to your posts and pages so readers can save, print, share or read your work offline, with one click.

The PDF looks like your website, not a plain text dump. Your theme's font sizes, colors, columns, galleries, tables and quotes carry over, so the document feels like part of your brand. The clutter stays behind: menus, sidebars, footers, share buttons and comment forms are removed, leaving only what matters.

= Perfect for =

* **Recipes** your visitors want to print and pin to the fridge.
* **Guides, tutorials and documentation** people keep for later.
* **Course material, handouts and worksheets** for students.
* **Reports, white papers and case studies** to share with a team.
* **Event programs, menus, price lists and product sheets.**
* **Articles and long reads** to enjoy offline or on an e-reader.

= Why Content PDF =

* **One click for your readers.** The button appears on the content types you choose. No sign-up, no waiting, no pop-ups.
* **Looks like your site.** Works with block themes and classic themes, and keeps the look of WordPress blocks.
* **Real, professional PDFs.** Text stays sharp and selectable, so readers can search, copy and highlight it.
* **Private by design.** The PDF is created right in the reader's browser. No third-party service, no API keys, no account, and your content never leaves your site.
* **Light on your server.** Your hosting does none of the heavy lifting, so even busy sites stay fast.
* **Simple settings.** Choose A4 or Letter paper, set the margins, show or hide the post title and featured image, and add a footer with page numbers.
* **Place the button anywhere.** Add it automatically, or drop the `[content_pdf_button]` shortcode wherever you like, with your own label.
* **You decide where pages break.** Add the `cpdf-page-break` class to any block to start it on a new page.
* **Always works.** If an older browser can't create the PDF, the print dialog opens instead, so nobody is left without a copy.
* **Respects private content.** Drafts and private posts stay visible only to people allowed to read them, and PDF pages are hidden from search engines.

= Made for designers and developers too =

Need a branded layout, a cover image or extra details such as prices or authors? Your theme can provide its own PDF template, for all content or for a single post type or post.

== Installation ==

1. In your dashboard, go to **Plugins → Add New**, search for "Content PDF" and click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
2. Go to **Settings → Content PDF**.
3. Choose where the button should appear (posts, pages or other content types), pick the paper size and margins, and save.

That's it. Visit any post and you'll see the "Download PDF" button.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need an account or an external service? =

No. Everything happens on your site and in your reader's browser. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay per download.

= Will the PDF match my theme? =

Yes. Content PDF uses your theme's styles, so headings, colors, columns, images and tables look the way they do on your site. Menus, sidebars and footers are left out.

= Can I add the button somewhere else? =

Yes. Use the `[content_pdf_button]` shortcode in any post, page or widget. Change the text with `[content_pdf_button label="Save as PDF"]`.

= Can visitors view the PDF in the browser instead of downloading it? =

Yes. Under **Settings → Content PDF**, set **Button action** to "Open the PDF in a new tab". The PDF then opens in the browser's PDF viewer. A single button can override the setting with `[content_pdf_button action="view"]` or `action="download"`.

= Can I add page numbers? =

Yes. Under **Settings → Content PDF**, the footer shows "Page 1 / 3" by default, and you can change the text.

= Does it work with pages and custom post types? =

Yes. Choose any public content type in the settings, such as posts, pages, products or recipes.

= Can readers copy text from the PDF? =

Yes. The text is real text, not an image, so it can be selected, searched and copied.

= What about videos and embeds? =

Videos and embedded content can't play on paper, so they are replaced with a link to the original.

= Is it safe for private content? =

Yes. Drafts and private posts can only be downloaded by people who are allowed to read them, and PDF pages are never indexed by search engines.
