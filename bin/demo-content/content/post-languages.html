The PDF uses the bundled Noto Sans for all text, which covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. Bold is real bold; italic is slanted synthetically.

Latin with diacritics

Català: L’àvia va menjar una poma i va beure aigua de la font.
Español: El pingüino ñandú comió jamón en el año pasado.
Français: Ça a été une très belle journée à l’hôtel, où l’œuvre était exposée.
Deutsch: Größere Bücher über Straßenbahnen fürs Büro.
Polski: Zażółć gęślą jaźń.
Português: A ação foi rápida e não houve confusão.

Greek

Ξεσκεπάζω την ψυχοφθόρα βδελυγμία.

Weights and styles

Regular text, bold text, italic text and bold italic text.

Typographic characters

“Curly quotes”, ‘single quotes’, «guillemets», en dash – em dash — ellipsis… bullet • copyright © registered ® trademark ™ section § degree ° euro € pound £ yen ¥ fractions ½ ¼ ¾ arrows → ← ↑ ↓ math ± × ÷ ≤ ≥ ≠.

Not covered

Scripts outside the bundled font (for example Chinese 中文, Japanese 日本語, Arabic العربية or emoji 🎉) are not in Noto Sans, so they are missing from the PDF. They are left here on purpose to show that limitation.
